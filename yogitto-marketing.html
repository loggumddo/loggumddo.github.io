YOGITTO

A simple marketing page for App Store listing use. YOGITTO helps people organize fridge ingredients, check expiration dates, and make better use of the food they already have.

What YOGITTO helps with

  • Track ingredients stored in the fridge
  • Check expiration dates at a glance
  • Reduce food waste from forgotten items
  • Plan shopping with a clearer view of what is already at home

Available languages on this page

Korean, English, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese.

Smarter ingredient management for your fridge

English

YOGITTO is an ingredient management app that helps you organize what is in your fridge and use food more wisely.

  • Organize fridge ingredients in one place.
